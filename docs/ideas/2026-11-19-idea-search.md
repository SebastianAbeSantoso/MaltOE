# Idea search, 2026-11-19 (Resurrection and Frankenstein)

Two research sub-agents each screened about 30 candidates against the builder's filters (visible wow in 5 s, a product not a library, not already shipped, no trade-off pitch, old tech at the core or genuinely clashing parts, buildable solo in 14 days with a one-day kill test). Prior art was found through web search snippets; "not found" means unverified.

Kiroween 2026 entries already seen (crowded): Teletext (Page 100, teletorch), HyperCard Revival, Strip (Cauzin Softstrip), afterlight-bbs, Signal Box, PhantomForge, FrankenGuard, Haunted API House.
